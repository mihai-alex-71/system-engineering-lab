## Bins (width 10 days)

| Age (days) | Defects |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | 3 |
| 11–20 | 11 |
| 21–30 | 9 |
| 31–40 | 4 |
| **Total** | **27** |

Mean age is 20.9 days and the median is 20 days (min 1, max 39).

## Conclusions

1. **Bulk:** 20 of 27 defects (74%) stay open between 11 and 30 days. The peak is at 11–20 days.
   Only 3 defects are fixed in under 10 days.
2. **Tail:** there is a right tail of 4 defects that live more than 30 days (32–39 days). All 4 are
   among the earliest reported (Feb 1 – Feb 7).
3. **Explanation:** fixes are delivered in batches, not continuously. 24 of 27 defects are closed on just
   3 dates (9 on Mar 1, 8 on Mar 3, 7 on Mar 12), which look like release or build dates. A defect's age
   therefore depends mostly on how long it waits for the next release. Defects reported early in February
   wait the longest, and that wait is the tail.
4. **Improvement:** more frequent releases (continuous integration) and triage of old defects would
   shorten the tail and the average age.
